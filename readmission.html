Explainability / SHAP

Explainability turns scores into clinical action.

Black-box predictions do not change behavior. Patient-level drivers do.

  • 4 prior inpatient stays -> +0.18 risk
  • Discharged to SNF -> +0.12 risk
  • Insulin dose changed -> +0.07 risk
  • Age 30-40 -> -0.04 risk
  • Short LOS (1 day) -> -0.03 risk
